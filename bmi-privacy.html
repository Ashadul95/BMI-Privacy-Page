2. Information entered into the app

Wellness records

You may enter weight, height, BMI results, goals, hydration, steps, sleep, mood, profile information, plan progress, and achievement progress. The current app stores these records locally on the device using local app storage. The current app does not provide account sync.

Exports and sharing

When you request CSV export, PDF export, or result sharing, the app creates the requested file or text and invokes the operating-system share sheet. The destination is chosen by you. The app does not automatically upload exported files.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Bodywise requests notification permission and schedules a local daily check-in notification. You can cancel scheduled reminders from Settings. Notification content is generated by the app.

5. How information is used

  • To calculate and display BMI.
  • To show wellness trends, goals, reminders, insights, plans, and achievements.
  • To create exports when you request them.
  • To operate advertising and measure ad delivery where applicable.
  • To maintain app reliability and respond to support requests.

Bodywise does not sell your manually entered wellness records.

6. Retention, deletion, and security

Local records remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. The current app provides a local history-clear action. For publisher-held support information or data processed by third-party SDKs, contact the publisher using the address above and follow the relevant provider’s deletion controls.

No storage or transmission method is risk-free. Keep your device secured and do not enter information you do not want stored on it.

7. Children

8. Health disclaimer

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. Bodywise does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure disease. It does not replace a qualified healthcare professional. If you feel unusually fatigued, dizzy, unwell, or have urgent concerns, seek medical care.
